# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Transmark to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Assume good faith; disagree on ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a private
communication to the maintainers (see the repository's contact info on
GitHub). Maintainers will review and respond to all reports, and may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that
violate this Code of Conduct.
